# Part V: The People {.part}

> The agents write the code. People still decide what is worth writing.

Everything so far has been about machines: terminals, loops, keys, wallets, GPUs. This last part is about the humans in the middle of it. What should you learn now, how do you get hired, how do you hire, and what does a working week look like when most of the typing is done by something else? We finish with the playbook we actually use.
