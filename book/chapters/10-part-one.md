# Part I: The Shift {.part}

> The keyboard was never the job. It was just the slowest part of it.

For fifty years the cost of software was dominated by the cost of writing it. That cost is collapsing. Part I looks at where the bottleneck went, what a team looks like when one engineer directs a fleet of agents, and the specific ways agents fail so you can build the guardrails before you need them.
